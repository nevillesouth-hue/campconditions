# Digest layout rules from the 26 Nov 2026 review

These belong in the Thursday draft templates and in next week's preview for Micky. Nothing here is a send instruction.

- Subjects are plain text (`digest_layout.plain_subject`). Never ship `&amp;` or other HTML entities in a subject.
- Broadcast footers use `{{{RESEND_UNSUBSCRIBE_URL}}}` and the label "Unsubscribe from this digest". Do not substitute `https://campconditions.au/unsubscribe/` (the form page). Resend Broadcasts already add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. Do not change live Resend account or domain settings for this.
- Open with a short three-line "What matters this weekend" summary. Keep later weather and Coming up sections tight.
- Tasmania alerts should show official PWS/TasALERT dates and detail, plus a one-line official Bookeasy camper action when that incident is still current.
- South Australia: keep the Innamincka flood caveat high, and trim repeated per-region weather lines.
- Show the Chip in ask once: the tip block only, not also in the top grey line.
