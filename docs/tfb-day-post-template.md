# Total Fire Ban day post

Template only. Fill the placeholders from that day's official Total Fire Ban notice. Do not add ratings, warnings, camp closures or district names that are not on that notice.

Placeholders:

- `{state}` — Victoria, New South Wales, South Australia or Tasmania. Queensland does not use the term Total Fire Ban. For a Queensland local fire ban, use the Queensland Fire Department notice instead of this template.
- `{districts}` — the districts named on the official notice. Write "statewide" only when the notice says the whole state.
- `{date}` — the date the ban applies, in the form on the official notice.
- `{official_tfb_url}` — the official Total Fire Ban page for that state, not a CampConditions page.

## Facebook

```
Total Fire Ban, {state}, {date}.

Districts: {districts}.

Check the official notice before you travel: {official_tfb_url}

What a Total Fire Ban means for camping: https://campconditions.au/fire-season/

CampConditions.au is not an official warning service. In an emergency call 000.
```
